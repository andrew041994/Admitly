# Admin and support SOP

## Identity and access

- Use individual administrator accounts with verified email. Never share sessions or copy bearer tokens into tickets or chat.
- Grant the least privilege needed, review access monthly, and remove access immediately when a role ends.
- Search by order/event reference when possible. Avoid copying personal data outside approved systems.

## Handling a case

1. Open or locate the support case and record the requester, channel, UTC time, affected event/order/ticket reference, and stated issue.
2. Verify the requester using existing account and transaction context. Do not ask for passwords, one-time codes, full payment credentials, or ticket QR payloads.
3. Inspect audit history, order/payment status, ticket state, transfers, check-in attempts, messages, and relevant request ID.
4. Explain the next action before taking it. High-impact actions—manual payment verification, refunds, financial adjustments, event cancellation, ticket voids, or check-in override—require the designated second approver.
5. Record the reason and evidence in the admin audit trail. Do not silently edit financial or admission state.
6. Send only the minimum necessary response, classify the case, and set an owner and follow-up time.

Escalate suspected account takeover, leaked ticket codes, payment mismatch, mass delivery failure, unsafe events, or administrator misuse to the incident process immediately.
